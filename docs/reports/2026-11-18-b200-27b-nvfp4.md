# Qwen3.8-27B NVFP4 B200 retest

FlashInfer attention with CUTLASS NVFP4 GEMM passed all 28 sampled
distribution checks at the original `p < 0.001` cutoff. Both serving lanes
passed mixed requests, cancellation, pool reuse and same-route replay.
Cross-route greedy token identity passed for two of seven prompts.

The earlier [RTX investigation](2026-11-18-main-deployment-and-nvfp4.md)
established that target logits diverged before sampling. This run reproduced
the failed AR versus DFlash2 sampling comparison on one NVIDIA B200 and
isolated the contributions of sampling, Gated DeltaNet and FP8 KV attention.

## Environment

- B200 SM100, driver 595.112.07, SGLang 0.5.23, PyTorch 2.14.1+cu130,
  FlashInfer 0.7.2 and Python 3.12.3.
- `RadixArk/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` at
  `319f741cce68d7914884900c138a1fbb70a42f30` uses ModelOpt mixed
  precision with NVFP4 MLP weights and a default FP8 E4M3 target KV cache.
  `incoai/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2` at
  `015e795645c74b1a0eeef3b570031fb62e769bc5` supplies the selector draft.
- Serving checks ran with decode graphs, two slots, 4,096 context tokens
  and an 8,192-token pool. Single-request greedy generation used tree
  rounds on the DFlash2 server. Sampled generation used width-eight chain
  rounds. Each sample cohort used 500 responses per prompt, four fixed
  prompts, six output tokens, temperature 1, top-k 20 and top-p 0.95.
  Server RNG seeds were distinct within each comparison.

## Faults isolated and changes

SGLang's DFlash2 selector passes its proposal probabilities to the DSpark
rejection kernel. Its target policy previously applied top-p after top-k
renormalization. FlashInfer AR uses the intersection of both cutoffs on the
original probabilities. The opt-in `AX_DFLASH_JOINT_SAMPLING=1` changes
the target policy and its sampling-mask capture for `FC1_DFLASH_TREE` chain
rounds when both cutoffs are active and min-p is disabled. The CUDA fixture
compares support and frequencies with the real AR sampler on fixed inputs.

On identical committed prefixes, packed AR GDN differed from chain verify
at all 36 incremental rows in the first layer. Unpacked AR GDN matched chain
verify at all 36 incremental rows at each of the first three layers. With
Triton full attention, all 36 identical-input rows at the first full-attention
layer still diverged, with maximum output difference 0.16406. AR reads both
prefix and current token through the FP8 KV cache, while the Triton block
verify path uses cache for the prefix and directly consumes the current
block's K and V. The checkpoint enables the FP8 cache by default.

FlashInfer paged attention reads the stored KV cache for AR and target
verification. In the eager probe, 4 of 42 identical-input incremental rows
at the first full-attention layer differed, with maximum difference 0.00781.
The raw softmax total variation over the first six outputs of the four
sampling prompts fell from a maximum of 0.12137 to 0.05901. All 48 matched
prefixes in this FlashInfer probe had the same top-logit token. The eager
probe used six prompts and at most eight output tokens. Its counts differ
between backends because the Triton routes diverged earlier.

The FlashInfer tree CUDA graph initially failed in SGLang 0.5.23's fast
planner because the plugin supplied a custom mask without the uniform
verify width. The plugin now supplies the width to upstream planners that
accept it and uses its existing packing path on earlier signatures.

## Serving and sampling comparisons

The Triton baseline reproduced the distribution failure. Using the joint
filter with unpacked AR improved the Triton result. Replacing the target KV
cache with BF16 on Triton still left failures. FlashInfer with FP8 KV reduced
the remaining failures to two related positions at chain width eight.

| AR versus DFlash2 profile | AR seed | DFlash2 seed | Distribution checks failed | Identical greedy runs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Triton, FP8 KV, packed AR, original filter, width 8 | 314160 | 271829 | 5 of 28 | 3 of 7 |
| Triton, FP8 KV, unpacked AR, joint filter, width 8 | 577215 | 161803 | 4 of 28 | 1 of 7 |
| Triton, BF16 KV, unpacked AR, joint filter, width 8 | 104729 | 104759 | 6 of 28 | 2 of 7 |
| FlashInfer, FP8 KV, unpacked AR, joint filter, width 8 | 104723 | 104761 | 2 of 28 | 2 of 7 |
| FlashInfer, FP8 KV, unpacked AR, joint filter, width 2 | 104723 | 130363 | 6 of 28 | 2 of 7 |
| FlashInfer, FP8 KV, CUTLASS NVFP4 GEMM, unpacked AR, joint filter, width 8 | 324161 | 324299 | 0 of 28 | 2 of 7 |

The cutoff for each distribution test was `p < 0.001`. Cross-route greedy
compares seven prompts with a 32-token output budget and a longest prompt of
2,266 tokens. Within each serving lane, 15 mixed requests passed and the
reference tokens were identical before and after 32 stress requests,
including cancellations and request-pool reuse.

In the width-eight FlashInfer run, positions four and five of the second
sampling prompt failed. The first four tokens were identical in all
responses. At the next position, the eager matching-prefix probe measured
raw top-token probability 0.89565 in AR and 0.95464 in chain verify. The
chain crosses top-p 0.95 and drops the second token. AR sampled it 54 of
500 times, versus zero occurrences in the chain cohort. Position five
inherits this difference from position four.

At chain width two, the same eager probe measured top-token probabilities
0.89565 in AR and 0.89372 in chain verify. The second prompt passed the
independent comparison, while the fourth prompt failed six tests. The two
widths have different numerical error on the same model and cache setup.

In the width-eight probe of the second prompt, the first observed activation
difference was at GDN layer 24 on the third incremental prediction, despite
identical layer input. At later positions the input to layer 25 matched
while its output still differed. Disabling fused decode projection and
convolution produced the same observed logits on this prefix.

With FlashInfer full attention and the CUTLASS NVFP4 GEMM backend, the
matched-prefix top probabilities for the same token were 0.89226 in AR
and 0.94740 in chain verify. Both retain the alternate candidate at
top-p 0.95. The eager cuDNN and TensorRT-LLM GEMM probes produced the same
probabilities on this prefix. CUTLASS was selected for the full retest with
fresh server seeds and the original width-eight chain.

The alternate token appeared in 51 of 500 AR responses and 27 of 500
DFlash2 responses. The affected position and its continuation each had
`p = 0.00668`. The pass is specific to the recorded prompts, sample count,
cutoff and B200 configuration. The model still shows numerical variation
between the two routes.

## Reproduce the CUTLASS retest

Run from the repository root. Set `runner_python` to the absolute path of
the GPU environment's Python and set `target_model` and `draft_model` to
the two pinned local checkpoint paths.

```bash
common=(--candidate-only --python "$runner_python" --baseline "$PWD" --candidate "$PWD"
        --model "$target_model" --draft "$draft_model"
        --attention-backend flashinfer --fp4-gemm-backend flashinfer_cutlass
        --max-tokens 32 --long-context 2600 --stress-requests 32
        --repeat-reference --sampling-n 500)
"$runner_python" scripts/check_model_regression.py "${common[@]}" \
  --route ar --gdn-unpacked-decode --server-seed 324161 --out results/nvfp4-cutlass-ar
"$runner_python" scripts/check_model_regression.py "${common[@]}" \
  --route tree --dflash-joint-sampling --chain-width 8 --server-seed 324299 \
  --out results/nvfp4-cutlass-tree
"$runner_python" benchmarks/sglang/sampling_dist_check.py compare \
  results/nvfp4-cutlass-ar/candidate/sampled.jsonl \
  results/nvfp4-cutlass-tree/candidate/sampled.jsonl --require-independent-seeds
```

The cache dtype follows checkpoint metadata. The target allocates FP8 E4M3
KV and the drafter allocates BF16 KV. The harness stores the launch flags,
sampling metadata, stress counters and reference tokens for each lane.

## Greedy follow-up

Greedy token identity requires autoregressive decoding (AR) and DFlash2 to
produce exactly the same sequence of token IDs from the same prompt. Each
route chooses a token with the highest logit at every step. If the routes
choose different tokens, the next step sees a different prefix, so later
tokens can diverge as well.

A bounded B200 rerun used the same seven tokenized prompts, CUTLASS profile,
FP8 target KV cache and CUDA graph configuration. Disabling tree rounds
with `AX_TREE=0` gave 2 of 7 greedy outputs identical to AR. Default
tree routing also matched 2 of 7, with different first-divergence positions.
SGLang's `--enable-deterministic-inference` yielded 2 of 7 matches in a
separate AR/tree comparison.

The tree verifier's pre-commit logits were captured at the sampler boundary.
All 217 emitted tree-verified tokens had maximal logits in their own verify
rows. Of the five first divergences against AR, three had opposite rankings
for the same top two token IDs and two resolved an AR tie differently.
The first divergence on prompt 0 occurred at output position 7 (zero-based).
The two routes still shared the same prefix at that step.

| Candidate token ID | AR logit | DFlash2 tree logit |
|---|---:|---:|
| `1590` | **30.125** | 29.250 |
| `25` | 29.375 | **29.750** |

AR selected token `1590` and DFlash2 selected token `25`. For DFlash2 to
reproduce AR greedily, the two target forward paths must select the same
token on each shared prefix. Use AR when exact AR output is required.

Launch manifests, raw traces and the commit audit were in the
`greedy-recheck/` subdirectory of that evidence directory, which was
removed with it. The test processes
exited and B200 allocation returned to 0 MiB.

The regression harness can check saved lanes and extract their first
divergences without a GPU. Supply the lane directories containing
`input_ids.json` and `tokens.json`.

```bash
python scripts/check_model_regression.py compare-greedy \
  --reference results/nvfp4-cutlass-ar/candidate \
  --candidate results/nvfp4-cutlass-tree/candidate \
  --out results/nvfp4-greedy-comparison.json
```

The JSON includes the exact shared prefix and both chosen token IDs for
each first divergence. A missing token ID means that route ended at that
position. The command exits with status 1 when any output differs.

### Recurrent GDN verification candidate

The SGLang 0.5.23 unpacked DECODE and TARGET_VERIFY calls specialize the same
Triton recurrent function with different flags for persistent-state writes,
intermediate snapshots and tree-parent lookup. The plugin's fused tree path
uses a separate closed-form calculation. An upstream report on the same model
family also observed recurrent drift in speculative verification.
[SGLang Qwen3.8 recurrent drift report](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/35150)

`AX_GDN_RECURRENT_VERIFY=1` runs the unpacked decode kernel once per candidate
step into scratch state and leaves the accepted-state copy to SGLang. Each tree
node reloads its parent's snapshot. Padding rows skip snapshot writes. Use
`FC1_FUSED=0` and `--linear-attn-verify-backend triton` with this mode so
SGLang owns convolution and state commit and dispatches to the replay kernel.
The model regression harness selects these flags with `--gdn-recurrent-verify`
on the DFlash2 lane. Compare against unpacked AR on the same CUDA host using
the original `input_ids.json` from the CUTLASS evidence.

```bash
"$runner_python" scripts/check_model_regression.py \
  --python "$runner_python" --baseline "$PWD" --candidate "$PWD" \
  --model "$target_model" --draft "$draft_model" \
  --baseline-route ar --route tree --gdn-unpacked-decode --gdn-recurrent-verify \
  --dflash-joint-sampling --attention-backend flashinfer --fp4-gemm-backend flashinfer_cutlass \
  --input-ids results/nvfp4-cutlass-ar/candidate/input_ids.json --max-tokens 32 \
  --out results/nvfp4-recurrent-greedy
```

`--route chain` forces chain rounds for the candidate, including on the
single-request greedy prompts. The harness saves the first divergence of
each output even when the greedy gate fails.

The CPU test covers chain and tree ancestry, scratch writes and isolation from
padding. The CUDA fixture `gpu_gdn_recurrent_check.py` compares replayed
verification against independent unpacked decode at the output and snapshot
boundaries. The [RTX PRO 6000 follow-up](2026-11-19-rtx-27b-nvfp4.md) passed
the CUDA fixture. End-to-end greedy identity remains open.
