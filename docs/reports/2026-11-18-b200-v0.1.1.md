# v0.1.1 Flash Next verification on B200 (2026-11-18)

The installed `ax-engine-sglang` v0.1.1 wheel was built from `fcc9366` and tested
on one NVIDIA B200 (SM100, 183,359 MiB, driver 595.112.07). The environment used
Python 3.12.3, SGLang 0.5.23, PyTorch 2.14.1+cu130, and FlashInfer 0.7.2. The 86
CPU tests passed on the pod and on Python 3.10, 3.12, and 3.14 locally. The schema
and the nine B200 CUDA groups passed. They include PLE gather, QSA negative
controls, stable Top-K replay, runtime rollback, tree masks, GDN state commits,
forward probes, and both sampling kernel controls.

The target is NVIDIA's Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4 revision
`fc694b54fb0174e0913e6adf86691ef85a4ead47`. All 11 checkpoint shards and the
verified compact MTP checkpoint were staged in RAM. Every benchmark lane used
Triton attention, the explicit FlashInfer TRT-LLM MoE backend, GPU-resident FP8
PLE, decode CUDA graphs, 16 active slots, and disabled radix cache and prefill
CUDA graphs. The launch records confirm that PLE offload was disabled. Stock
MTP3 used the native ring and MTP7 used the plugin's ring of 16 slots.

## Throughput and time to first token

Two passes used the same ordered sets of 24 code, math, and chat prompts at
concurrency 1, 4, and 16. Requests were greedy with thinking disabled and a
256-token output limit. Prompt lengths ranged from 22 to 203 tokens. The
benchmarker verified complete streams and recorded an ordered prompt digest
per workload. Aggregate throughput includes prompt processing and queueing.

## Serving and output checks

All four initial serving lanes passed 15 mixed requests each. Stock MTP3
reproduced six short greedy references and eager MTP7 reproduced all seven.
The benchmark configurations for AR and MTP7 both reproduced the six short
references and differed on the 2,609-token input after mixed requests. The
eager MTP7 lane used deterministic inference and stable QSA Top-K. Its last
diagnostic count showed 4,000 checked groups and zero corrupt groups.

The follow-up enabled `SGLANG_PLUGINS=ax-engine`, `AX_QSA_STABLE_TOPK=1`, and
`--enable-deterministic-inference` for both routes. MTP7 also used
`AX_QSA_RING=16`. With two active slots and CUDA graphs enabled, each route
passed 15 mixed requests and 32 stress requests from eight clients. Each
observed five cancellations and zero request errors, returned its pools to idle,
and reproduced all seven greedy references. MTP7 graph and eager outputs
matched on every reference. Stable AR and stable MTP7 matched on four of seven
references across routes.

Use the stable configuration when repeating long-context requests on this
setup. On B200 the tested launch arguments explicitly select
`--no-ple-offload-embedding`, `--moe-runner-backend flashinfer_trtllm`,
`--speculative-moe-runner-backend flashinfer_trtllm`, and
`--json-model-override-args '{"text_config":{"ple_embedding_dtype":"float8_e4m3fn"}}'`.
The RTX PRO 6000 correctness campaign used pinned-host PLE. Its controlled
throughput run was stopped before measurement for pod shutdown.
[RTX QA report](2026-11-18-development-qa.md)

The independent B200 comparison of AR and MTP7 used 2,000 samples per lane,
500 for each of four prompts, at temperature 1, top-p 0.95 and top-k 20. The
server seeds were 314159 and 271828. Nine of 28 tests fell below α = 0.001.
Cross-route NVFP4 sampled equivalence failed on this workload.

The initial matched-prefix probe let the target and MTP drafter overwrite
one another's exports when both ran in the same process. `ae9790e` excludes
draft inputs and uses unique capture paths. Six GPU probe tests passed, and
the corrected eager capture kept all ten prompts in each of the three lanes.

| Comparison | Identical prefixes | Different target argmax | Largest absolute logit difference | Largest raw-softmax total variation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AR vs. stock MTP3 | 209 | 5 | 4.84375 | 0.2770 |
| AR vs. ring-16 MTP7 | 223 | 2 | 5.296875 | 0.3010 |

All matched prefill logits were identical. Differences appeared before
sampling in both the stock and patched MTP paths during later decoding. Each
of the 720 committed tokens had zero regret against its own route's target
logits. The comparison locates a difference before sampling without
attributing the full distribution failure to any single kernel.

Exact AR-equivalent outputs and sampled distributions remain unqualified for
Flash Next NVFP4 MTP. Flash Next tree and DFlash2 support still depend on PLE,
QSA tree handling and auxiliary hidden-state capture. [Feasibility record](../decisions/2026-11-17-qwen38-flash-next-feasibility.md)
