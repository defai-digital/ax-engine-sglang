# NCM implementation lessons: muse

## 1. Official NCM recipe vs `phase1/vqvae.py`

**Measured (paper + code).** NCM stage 1 is a multiscale residual VQ-VAE with one codebook per level, nested dropout, and a token-level cross-entropy reconstruction objective ([blog](https://hazyresearch.stanford.edu/blog/2026-10-12-ncm)). Base vocabs are small: GPT-2 50,304 for XSum/OWT, BERT-uncased 30,528 for LM1B ([repo](https://github.com/HazyResearch/ncm)). Block lengths are 48 (XSum), 64 (WMT14), 128 (LM1B/TinyStories), 1024 (OWT) ([audit-blog](https://www.falsegreen.dev/posts/the-most-interesting-checkpoint-is-five-bytes-long/), [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). Hierarchies are deep: XSum 9 levels 4→48, OWT 32 levels (squares 1→1024), LM1B 12 levels 1→128 ([repo](https://github.com/HazyResearch/ncm), [audit-blog](https://www.falsegreen.dev/posts/the-most-interesting-checkpoint-is-five-bytes-long/)). Codebooks are 4096/level (XSum) or 8192/level (OWT, LM1B) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1), [repo](https://github.com/HazyResearch/ncm)). Tokenizer width is d=384; the encoder is by default just embedding lookup (`enc_layers=0`), decoder 1 Block (4 for LM1B) ([repo](https://github.com/HazyResearch/ncm)). Generators are 12×768 (~88–120M); largest trained is 725M ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1), [audit-blog](https://www.falsegreen.dev/posts/the-most-interesting-checkpoint-is-five-bytes-long/)). Tokenizer training exposure is 61.7B positions (OWT: 78,432×768×1024) and 6.8B (LM1B); generator updates 50k–350k at batch 256–2048, LR 3e-4→3e-5 ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). Tricks: EMA 0.99, commitment 0.25, eps 1e-5; dead-code restart default 0.0 (off); entropy loss not found in code; k-means init exists but is off in eval configs; pre-quant GroupNorm (affine=False); pre-quant dropout 0.25 (XSum); learned L1 cascade sampler; quant_resi 0.5 non-shared; straight-through at level 0 only ("higher levels diverge within ~100 steps"); fine-drop p=0.5, uniform cutoff, min_keep=1 (LM1B dual-loss 0.7/0.3) ([repo](https://github.com/HazyResearch/ncm), [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)).

**Reconstruction (measured).** OWT 99.99%, Sudoku 100%, others "close to 100%"; codebook utilization 62–90%; all K-ladder tokenizers ≥99.95% ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). Errors hit names, numerals, diacritics, markup ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)).

**Ablations (measured).** More levels (K=4/8/16/32, 1→128, 4096 codes, 40k+10k steps, no corruption) lowers GenPPL with diminishing returns ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). Six schedule shapes: numbers are figure-only, not found in text ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). Block-diagonal attention wins (best row 15.41 PPL, 5.65 s/1024 samples; 1.21–1.40× faster) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). T5-small context encoder gains only +0.10/+0.15/+0.24 ROUGE at +35.3M params, rejected ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). Loss weight α≤1 flat (18.9–19.0), α=1.5 worse (19.4) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). Moderate corruption helps, excess hurts (figure-only numbers) ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). CFG peaks at w=0.5 (ROUGE) / 1.0 (BLEU); MBR +4.48 R-1 from 1→256 candidates ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). **Limitations listed:** fixed K and level lengths, decoupled tokenizer, ≤725M scale, coarse-transition artifacts ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)).

**Differences that could explain your 66–84% (inference from the above).** Your vocab is 248k vs their ≤50k ([vqvae](/Volumes/Ext16TR0/code/ax-engine-sglang/phase1/vqvae.py)); block 32 with K=4–6 vs their 48–1024 with K=9–32; LayerNorm vs their GroupNorm pre-quant; no pre-quant dropout vs 0.25; fixed area/linear resampling vs learned L1 cascade; global straight-through vs level-0-only (their comment predicts your divergence risk); restarts on (0.05–1.0) vs default off; tied 248k embeddings vs their separate decoder head; ~2.4B training positions vs their ~62B (OWT); dropout 0 vs 0.1–0.3.

## 2. Independent reproductions

**Measured.** An audit rescored 128 supplied XSum summaries at 34.73/12.22/27.89 vs paper 35.0/12.8/28.0 with matching hashes, but did not regenerate them; Sudoku (99.4/96.4/86.3) and LM1B (PPL 89.34 vs 93.05) are unrescored execution-team reports ([audit](https://jbsilver96.github.io/data/ncm-audit.html)). A GTX-1070 run scored 33.0/12.3/26.4 on 64 docs at 0.34 s/summary, and notes the release is eval-only, `.cuda()`-hardwired, `weights_only=False`, the OWT checkpoint is a 5-byte TODO, and speed claims are vs diffusion LMs, never vs AR ([audit-blog](https://www.falsegreen.dev/posts/the-most-interesting-checkpoint-is-five-bytes-long/)). An Apple-Silicon port runs TinyStories+Sudoku ([port](http://dev.to/janmusic/running-released-next-concept-modeling-checkpoints-on-apple-silicon-28mi)); a 725M chat fine-tune exists ([ckpt](https://huggingface.co/banan332/WB-catboy-NCM-Instruct-725M)). Reddit copies were reported inaccessible ([post](https://www.pinoytoolbox.org/post/on-next-concept-modeling-ncm)); forum discussion is mostly off-topic ([thread](https://daily.dev/posts/next-concept-modeling-fast-parallel-coarse-to-fine-text-generation-qtgfw9wdj)). GitHub issues/discussions, OpenReview comments, and any 100k+-vocab, Qwen-space, code, or long-document reproduction: not found. **Inference:** no independent party has validated NCM outside its shipped small-vocab boxes.

## 3. Related lines

**Measured.** NCP-ArchPreview (8.9B, 5.73T tokens) adds product-quantized next-concept prediction beside NTP, reaching the baseline's final loss with 51.3% of tokens and +2.45 macro (+5.99 GSM8K) ([report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10715), [digest](https://aidigest.shelfcritter.com/digest/2026-09-12)). Meta LCMs predict next sentence-SONAR-embeddings (200 languages), diffusion variant best ([ref](https://www.ultralytics.com/glossary/large-concept-models-lcms)). VAR uses next-scale prediction for images ([survey](https://dev.clauneck.workers.dev/fulviojorge/next-concept-modeling-ncm-a-new-approach-to-ai-language-generation-44gg)); NCM's paper states text lacks image downsampling's natural coarse levels ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1)). HSD (arXiv 2601.05724) is lossless hierarchical verification, +12% over EAGLE-3 ([abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05724)). DSpine: Qwen3-8B acceptance 3.77→4.82 (+27.8%), +23.3% throughput vs DFlash; 27B agent tasks to 3.84× AR ([bench](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/tencent-dspine-speculative-decoding-drafter-qwen3-8b-27-percent-longer-acceptance-vs-dflash-2026), [bench2](https://www.besthub.dev/articles/dspine-depth-wise-causal-injection-for-parallel-speculative-decoding-4x-sglang-throughput-06e818a1da60)). JetSpec: 9.64× MATH-500, 4.58× chat (Qwen3-8B, H100, greedy, budget 256) ([lab](https://haoailab.com/blogs/parallel-tree-decoding/)). JetSpec++: 8.94 vs 6.89 accepted tokens, to 7.45× wall-clock ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.19792v2)). SpecPilot: 10.87× on Qwen3.8-27B ([rep](https://www.besthub.dev/articles/specpilot-10-87x-llm-inference-speedup-via-cost-model-guided-speculative-decoding-3be806221bef)). SpecOPD: +41.45% acceptance, 3.56× AR on 27B ([rep](https://dev.to/olaughter/branch-parallel-draft-distillation-speeds-text-generation-36x-1efk)). D-REAL: +0.23 tokens after one epoch ([abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10432)). Residual-VQ text autoencoder results beyond NCM/HDLM: not found. **Inference:** every winning drafter optimizes acceptance length with ~1-forward parallel drafts plus causal conditioning; none uses a VQ hierarchy; your 7-pass non-causal drafter pays 7 forwards where they pay ~1.

## 4. Concept hierarchy as verified draft

**Measured.** PlanSpec feeds an NCM translation to an AR model as plan+draft: BLEU 22.7 vs 21.5/18.5 with 41% fewer forwards, but copies plan errors; a plan-following LoRA reaches 27.2 vs 24.5, and with prompt lookup 0.45 forwards/token and 1.5× wall-clock (28.3 vs 25.5 BLEU); precision gating cuts 26% of forwards with 6/100 number errors vs 11–12; NCM writes a 64-token block 6.7× faster than same-size AR but loses names ([study](http://zenodo.org/records/23607317)). A lossless NCM/VQ draft verified by exact-match AR prefix: not found. NCP's report mentions a speculative-decoding study ([note](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2609.10715)), details not found. **Inference:** plan-conditioned AR verified by AR is evidenced; VQ-code drafts verified losslessly are not — your reviewers' objection stands unrefuted.

## 5. Ranked changes

1. Switch to plan mode (final level predicts tokens); 2. add causal conditioning inside the draft; 3. train with NCM-style corruption (~0.5 mean, decreasing); 4. add copy-from-source for entities; 5. train against acceptance length, not token accuracy. Contradicted: ≥99% VQ on 248k vocab, 7 sequential passes, global straight-through with restarts.

| Change | Evidence | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Plan mode](http://zenodo.org/records/23607317) | PlanSpec: 27.2 vs 24.5 BLEU | Removes recon ceiling | Retrain head |
| [Causal draft](https://haoailab.com/blogs/parallel-tree-decoding/) | JetSpec 9.64×; DSpine +28% | Higher accept rate | Small arch edit |
| [Corruption](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1) | NCM F.3: moderate best | Closes 0.95→0.31 gap | Retrain only |
| [Copy entities](http://zenodo.org/records/23607317) | 6/100 vs 11–12 errors | Fixes names/numbers | Lookup + LoRA |
| [Accept loss](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10432) | +41%; +0.23 tok | Direct metric gain | Loss swap |
