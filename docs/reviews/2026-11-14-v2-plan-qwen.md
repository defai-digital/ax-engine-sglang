Volume: 1–2M tokens detects change; 5–10M for a reliable 27B gain; early‑stop on held‑out accepted length (AL) every 0.5M. **Do not make plain CE continued training the control recipe**: D‑REAL shows it *drops* τ at T=1 (DSpark 4.67→4.60, DFlash2 4.69→4.60) while its acceptance objective adds +0.07/+0.23 (arXiv 2611.10432). Prefer SpecForge online target (NCP used 5B×10 epochs online), or cache only the fused tap if DSpine consumes a DFlare‑style mix (~300 GB). Confirm what DSpine consumes first. **2. Concept source.** C1a beats C1b, but the plan misreads the evidence. NCP's +4.17% came from the target's *trained* concept module (k=4 mean‑pool → PQ softmax‑weighted continuous sum, MSE to stop‑grad), not raw pooled states (arXiv 2609.10715 §5.3, Table 9). C1a injects unstructured pooled states; C1b's VQ latent inherits your 66–84% tokenizer and the unresolved 0.06‑vs‑0.66 injection bug. Best third option **C1c**: anchor → small MLP → predict the *next chunk's mean‑pooled tapped state* (stop‑grad MSE), inject the prediction — NCP's mechanism minus the codebook, and the NCM "next concept" in continuous form. Also ablate the built‑in MTP‑head hidden state as a free concept proxy. Skip PQ codebooks (17M‑param adaptation, overkill). Controls: constant‑input (have it) **plus shuffled‑concept** (permute across examples); constant alone doesn't rule out generic regularization. Add a bitwise identity test: concept‑dropped ⇒ exact control output. Power: +3% on AL≈5 (Δ≈0.15), paired σ_d 0.5–1.0 ⇒ ~90–350 prompts; use **≥300 held‑out prompts/domain** (~100–300k tokens), cluster bootstrap by prompt (rounds correlate), 3 seeds, require 95% CI lower bound >0. Measure AL under *served dynamic verification*, not teacher‑forced (your chunks variant: TF 0.95 vs free‑run 0.31).
