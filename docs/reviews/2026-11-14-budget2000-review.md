# Review of routes for a 2000 GPU-hour budget — 2026-11-14
Brief: `docs/reviews/2026-11-14-budget2000-brief.md`. Answers of gpt-6.1-sol (codex), grok, muse and Qwen 4 Max preview, unedited apart from removed tool preambles. Kimi did not return a complete answer (its partial notes preferred a distilled smaller model with an existing drafter). After the brief was sent the owner proposed Qwen3.5-9B as the development scale; where the answers say 8B, read 9B.

## codex

1. **Choose R2, with roughly a 10% chance of meeting the complete bar within 2,000 GPU-hours.** These are subjective probabilities, conditional on one engineer and your measurements:

   - **R1: 2%.** [DFlare](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02091) tripled training samples, yet improved over DFlash by approximately 5–11%. Bigger drafters are credible incremental improvements; 3× requires a breakthrough, especially on chat.
   - **R2: 10%.** [Medusa-2](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.10774v3) establishes joint backbone/head training, reporting 2.3–2.8× over AR. It does **not** establish 3× over modern drafting.
   - **R3: <1%.** [NCM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14162) reports 10–31× over diffusion baselines on its text tasks. That provides no demonstrated bridge to 27B instruction quality.
   - **R4: <1% for your stated throughput bar.** [CODI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21074) reports 3.1× reasoning compression at GPT-2 scale. Fewer thinking tokens can improve answer latency without increasing emitted tok/s.
   - **R5: 3%—TiDAR-style integrated self-speculation.** [TiDAR](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08923v1) reports 4.71–5.91× over AR, but its 8B training used 150B tokens. That recipe exceeds this budget.

   These citations are verified. None demonstrates your requested outcome.

2. **R2 recipe: adapt the model’s continuation, then verify exactly against that adapted model.** Start from released DSpine architecture/checkpoints. Train its roughly 1–2B-parameter decoder plus rank-64 attention LoRA in the trunk, approximately 50–150M additional parameters. Share embeddings/output head; preserve causal trunk attention. The boundary representation sees only the committed prefix. No future-token leakage; no VQ initially.

   Generate 100M development tokens, then up to 500M target-model tokens: roughly equal code, mathematical reasoning, and conversation, with benchmark decontamination. Use four training passes, including refreshed adapted-model rollouts corrected or scored by the original teacher.

   Loss: trunk next-token CE + original-to-adapted KL + block-token CE backpropagated through boundary features + layer-wise decoder embedding supervision. Warm the decoder before enabling LoRA; use a lower trunk learning rate. These follow Medusa’s preservation strategy. Adopt [DSpine’s](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36173v2) predecessor curriculum: teacher predecessors initially, decaying to predicted predecessors. Add corrupted and self-generated prefixes.

   Schedule blocks 16→32→64. Start with one decoder pass; permit one refinement only if measured throughput improves. This combination is an experimental proposal, not a published validated recipe.

   **Main failure:** predictability improves by flattening reasoning or producing generic chat. Agreement rises while capability falls.

3. **Compute fits one serious attempt, not an open-ended search.** Planning assumptions: 150 effective TFLOP/s per GPU, 1,500 generated tokens/s through batching, checkpointing and chunked logits.

   Use conservative training arithmetic \(6PD\), plus \(2ND\) for original-teacher scoring:

   | Work | 8B validation | 27B campaign |
   |---|---:|---:|
   | Data generation | 20 h | 100–150 h |
   | Training + teacher scoring | 55 h | 850–1,050 h |
   | Evaluation/profiling | 25 h | 100 h |
   | Reruns/integration allowance | — | 350 h |

   Combined: **1,600–1,800 GPU-hours; 200–400 slack**. Training exposures are 400M at 8B and 2B at 27B. These are estimates, not measured throughput. Halving effective compute throughput breaks the budget. LoRA saves optimizer memory, not trunk backpropagation.

4. **8B gate: spend at most 100 GPU-hours.** Require ≥3× measured decoding throughput over the strongest public drafter **on each task**, with every quality score ≥98% of original: full HumanEval, full GSM8K, and fixed-judge, length-controlled AlpacaEval. Report paired uncertainty; 164 HumanEval problems cannot establish a tight noninferiority claim alone. **Stop if the speed/quality frontier misses this gate.** No honest small-scale metric guarantees transfer; accepted length alone is insufficient.

5. **27B pilot: cap the next tranche at 250 GPU-hours.** Require ≥2.5× measured speed on every task at the quality floor, with ≥3× reachable using *observed* acceptance and measured block latency. Otherwise stop. Your 22–25-token estimate holds only if round time stays fixed; larger blocks may require substantially more.

6. **GO—R2, initially for 100 GPU-hours only: it directly attacks the remaining bottleneck, but the full-budget success probability is still low.**

## grok

**1. Q1.** R2 is the only route with a non-round-off chance. Subjective P(hit ≥3× DFlash2 tok/s at ≤2% relative HE/GSM + one chat metric, 2000 GPU-h, 27B): **R1 2%, R2 12%, R3 1%, R4 3%, R5 (SpecPilot-class systems) 5%.** Averaged over code/math/chat you need ~22–25 committed tokens per ~15–17 ms round; chat is 3.27 today.

R1 is capped. DFlash2 is already 7.52/8 on code. DSpine vs DFlash2 at matched data is +4–10% accept length (arXiv 2609.36173; HF Open-PerfectBlend 7.83 vs 7.55). NCP concept injection +4.17% (2609.10715). D-REAL +0.23 tokens (2611.10432). Track B: even `ratio:0.1` is 1.25×/1.05× tokens/round at flat accuracy; `force:4` kills GSM 0.947→0.413. Perfect block-16 at 15 ms is ~2.1× DFlash2 code, below the 3× bar. Longer lossless blocks do not raise chat’s per-position match rate.

R2 is Fast-dLLM v2 / SDAR, not NCM. Fast-dLLM v2 (2509.26328): ~1B-token AR→block-diffusion convert, quality matched, **2.5× vs AR** (2.54× Qwen2.5-7B on A100). SDAR (2510.06303): ~50B-token convert, **2–4× vs AR**. Medusa-2 (Cai et al. 2024): 2.3–3.6× vs AR with joint trunk FT. All are vs AR (~5× short of 3× DFlash2, which is already 4.86× AR on code). Hardware allows 32 tokens / 15 ms (~4× DFlash2) if the adapted trunk actually commits that many. Published conversions do not. Chat entropy is the likely wall.

R3: NCM (2610.14162) is 25–130M, vs diffusion LMs (OWT Gen.PPL 83 vs data 14.2; WMT BLEU 22.6 vs AR 25.2). Outside: ncm-mlx 87M Gen.PPL 24 vs same-shape AR 11; MASSIVE-de EM 51 vs 57.6. LLaDA-8B took **0.13M H800-h / 2.3T tokens** (2502.09992). Dream convert used **580B** tokens. 2000 h cannot put a 1–8B NCM student next to Qwen3.8-27B at 2%.

R4: Coconut (2412.06769) GSM8K 34.1 vs CoT 42.9, 0.26s→0.09s on GPT-2. Visible tokens stay AR/drafted, so **tok/s vs DFlash2 does not 3×**. A 2026 secondary writeup of “FLaRe” claims 3.9× vs CoT at 97% of CoT on Llama-1B; I did not open a PDF.

R5 SpecPilot (2611.02501): 4.46×→10.87× AR lossless at batch 1 (~2.4× stock spec, **not** 3× DFlash2). JetSpec++ (2610.19792): 7.45× AR, 8.94 accept / 16.

**2. Q2. Recipe (R2).** Train: LoRA (r=16–64) on trunk attn/MLP of Qwen3-8B then Qwen3.8-27B; freeze the original as teacher; 4–8 layer parallel block decoder (~200–400M) on the trunk hidden state at block boundaries; optional 1 refinement pass. Data: packed self-distill of **original** greedy (thinking off) on code/math/chat, 1–2B tokens at 8B, 0.3–0.5B at 27B. Losses: (i) teacher CE on the committed block given boundary state, (ii) small KL to frozen teacher on the same prefixes, (iii) NCM-style **conditioning corruption** (paper: mean rate ~0.5; ncm-mlx: no corruption Gen.PPL ~94–98 vs ~46–48 with it), (iv) scheduled sampling of own blocks into the next trunk pass. Fast-dLLM complementary block mask. Block schedule 8→16→32. Decode: one decoder pass, one trunk pass over the committed block (free teacher check; reject/resample only if KL or task-probe trips). Failure mode: chat stays high-entropy, so you either keep ~3 tokens/round or you flatten the trunk and blow the chat metric; extra refinement eats the 15 ms budget.

**3. Q3.** 8B: data gen 1B tok @ ~300 tok/s ≈ 90 h; LoRA+decoder 2B tok @ ~20k tok/s ≈ 30 h; eval ≈ 20 h. 27B: data gen 0.5B @ ~100 tok/s ≈ 140 h; LoRA 1B @ ~5–8k tok/s ≈ 40–60 h; eval ≈ 50 h. **~400–700 h, slack ~1300 h** for ablations. Naive 5B of 27B tokens (~1400 h) plus thinking traces blows the budget; cap 27B generation.

**4. Q4.** 8B, ≤100 h, pre-declared: original-8B greedy vs adapted. **Go to 27B** if HE and GSM both have ≥16 committed tokens/round at ≤2% relative task loss **and** one chat metric ≤3% relative **and** measured tok/s ≥2× the best 8B public drafter on the same GPU. **27B pilot (~200 h):** same three metrics vs original-27B; go-full if tok/s ≥2.5× DFlash2 with the quality bar. **Stop the project** if 8B cannot beat Track B (`ratio:0.1` 1.25×/1.05×) at 2% loss, or HE/GSM committed tokens/round stay <12, or chat falls >5% to buy length. That 8B miss predicts 27B will not reach 22–25.

**5. Q5.** **Go R2, kill at the 8B gate.** 2000 h buys the convert; it does not buy 3× DFlash2 unless the 8B numbers already show ~16+ tokens/round at the quality bar.

## muse

1. Q1 R1 bigger lossless drafter: 2%. Ceiling is block+1 and target entropy, not training. You need 22-25 committed/round; chat is 3. EAGLE-3 (2503.01840, real): up to 6.5x over AR best-case, ~2.4x in SGLang docs; current DFlash2 is already 4.9x over AR on code, so 3x over DFlash2 is ~14x over AR, above every lossless claim. DSpine/JetSpec++/SpecOPD numbers in repo notes (+27% accept length, 8.94 tok/round, +41%) are unverified post-cutoff citations, but all are still <half of 22.
2. Q1 R2 draftable trunk: 8-10%, best of the bad options. It removes the identity constraint, so the entropy ceiling can move. Bounds: Medusa (2401.10774, real) 2.3-3.6x over AR; CLLM Jacobi training (Kou et al. 2024, 2403.00835 per search) 2.4-3.4x over AR; Jacobi Forcing 3.8x on code "minimal loss" (2512.14681, unsure real). All are over AR, not over best drafter; none shows 3x over a 4.9x-over-AR drafter at 27B quality. Chat 3 to 22 is a 7x predictability jump.
3. Q1 R3 native NCM student: ~1%. NCM paper (2610.14162, unverified, from repo): 25-130M, OWT GenPPL 83 vs data 14.2, WMT BLEU 22.6 vs AR 25.2. Outside repros in repo: 87M NCM GenPPL 24.4 vs same-size AR 11.5; 12M 47.9 vs 14.7 despite 99.8% codec. Same-size AR beats NCM. A 1-8B student holding 27B quality on 2000h is fantasy.
4. Q1 R4 latent reasoning: ~2%. Coconut (2412.06769, real): GSM8K 34.1% vs CoT 42.9% (-20%), 0.09s vs 0.26s, and only thinking tokens shrink; visible answer still AR/drafted. CODI (2502.21074, fairly confident real): ~43.7 vs 44.1 on GPT-2/GSM8K, small-model only. Cannot give 3x tok/s at <=2% loss.
5. Q1 R5: only credible R5 is "smaller distilled AR + best drafter" (e.g. 8B + DSpine) instead of 27B. ~15% if model switch is allowed, ~3% under same-target rule. 8B is ~3x less bandwidth so 2-3x over 27B+drafter is plausible on code, but Qwen3-8B vs 27B gap on chat/hard tasks exceeds 2%. System autotune (SpecPilot 10.87x claim, unverified) is not NCM and does not fix chat entropy.
6. Q2 recipe for R2: train LoRA-or-full trunk (rank 32-64, or full 27B if budget allows) jointly with a 2-4 layer block decoder (~0.5-1B params at 27B, init from trunk hidden-state projection; phase2 shows encoder-state init 0.65 to 2.15, largest gain). Data: 0.5-2B tokens of 27B self-distilled greedy responses, code/math/chat mix. Losses: block NLL + AR distill NLL on clean targets + consistency loss on own Jacobi rollouts. Practices that matter: conditioning corruption with linearly decreasing rate (NCM F.3), rollout/scheduled-sampling distillation not pure teacher forcing (CLLM/Jacobi), 1-2 intra-block refinement passes (JetSpec++), prefix-weighted loss 0.8^j (phase2 0.45 to 0.65), block schedule 8 to 16 to 32.
7. Q2 main failure mode: error compounding plus chat entropy. Your decisive run proves it: force-4 already 0.413/0.628 accuracy, top-2 accept 0.523/0.695. Training must lift the whole force-commit floor, not just stretch relaxed acceptance 1.05-1.25x. Likely outcome is either still ~4-6 tok/round on chat or a diversity collapse that fails the chat metric.
8. Q3 arithmetic (rough, batch-1 inference, BF16, 30-40% MFU): data gen dominates. 27B drafted ~400 tok/s means 1B tokens ~700 GPU-h; 8B drafted ~1000 tok/s means 1B ~280h. Training: 8B ~12k tok/s/GPU, 1B tokens ~25h; 27B ~3.5k tok/s, 1B ~80h; LoRA ~0.6x. Eval rounds ~10-20h each. 2000 fits: 8B full loop ~300-400h; 27B pilot on 1-2B tokens ~800-1500h total. Slack ~30-50% if 27B needs <=2B tokens; zero slack if it needs >3B or full-pretrain-scale data.
9. Q4 8B gate (<=100h): train block-16 R2 on 100-200M self-distilled 8B tokens; metric is committed tok/round at <=2% relative loss vs 8B AR greedy, averaged over held-out code/math/chat. Go: >=1.8x exact-DSpine average AND chat >=6 tok/round. This is the minimum slope that could extrapolate to 3x at 27B with 10x more data.
10. Q4 stop rule and 27B pilot: stop the project if 8B gives <1.3x average, or chat <4.5 tok/round, or any domain exceeds 2% loss before 1.5x, or force-8 accuracy does not close >=50% of the gap to exact. 27B pilot gate: LoRA + decoder on 200-300M tokens; need >=2x DFlash2 tok/round on code+math at <=2% loss to continue spending.
11. Q5: go R2 at 8B only to the gate, else stop; nothing else has a published path from 3 to 22 tokens per round, and R2 probably fails too.

## qwen

**1. Q1：機率與 published 上限**
殘酷座標：無損前沿 JetSpec++（2610.19792）accept 8.94、GB200 單 req 1361 tok/s、7.45×AR；SpecPilot（2611.02501）10.87×AR（batch1、無損、系統搜尋）。lossy adaptation 前沿 Fast-dLLM v2（2509.26328，review 引用）~1B tokens 僅 **2.5×AR——比你們 DFlash2 的 4.86×AR 還慢**。bar＝3×DFlash2≈14×AR、chat 需 ~12 committed tokens/round；若「best public drafter」算 JetSpec++，code 目標更貼近其 1361。**沒有任何 published 結果落在 bar 的 3× 以內。**
- **R1 ~1%**：SpecOPD（2611.02846）上限 3.56×AR、D-REAL（2611.10432）+0.23 token、DSpine（2609.36173）+27.8% accept。chat accept 被 target 熵鎖在 3–4；code 要 22–25，published 最高 8.94。
- **R2 ~5%**（全 bar 含 chat 實為 2–3%）：唯一攻擊「序列 commitment＋target 熵」的路線。Fast-dLLM v2 證明 AR→block 可保品質但只 2.5×AR；要 14×AR 需 full-block commit 16–64 且 ≤2% loss，比 published 高 5.6×。你們 force_4→GSM8K 0.413 是失敗前兆。
- **R3 <1%**：NCM（2610.14162）25–130M、Gen.PPL 83 vs data 14.2；ncm-mlx 87M Gen.PPL 24.4 vs AR 11.48、MASSIVE-de EM 51 vs 57.6；公開記錄**沒有任何 NCM code model／HumanEval 數字**。27B→8B gap＋generator gap＋pretrain-scale 資料，2000h 不够迭代。
- **R4 ~2%（metric 錯位）**：Coconut（2412.06769）GSM8K 34.1 vs CoT 42.9（~20% rel loss）、0.26→0.09s 是 answer-latency ~3×，減少 token 數而非加速 visible tok/s，達不了 stated bar。
- **R5 我更信：stop／重談 bar。** 真正「MUCH faster」前沿是 SpecPilot 式系統最佳化（非 NCM）或 27B→3B 窄域 specialist＋drafter；都不是「27B 品質＋conc-1 tok/s 3×」。

**2. Q2：R2 recipe**
訓練：trunk LoRA r64–128（attn+MLP，~0.5–1% 參數）或 8B 全 FT；0.5–1B 平行 block decoder（DSpine 式）condition on boundary hidden state＋position emb，一次出 B token；可選 k-latent concept bottleneck。
資料：1–2B tokens **trunk 自身 greedy 續寫**（self-distillation，own outputs 解 exposure bias），code/math/chat 混合。
Loss：masked-block CE（LLaDA/BD3 式）＋corruption＋self-conditioning；對 frozen 原 trunk 的 AR anchor/KL 防漂移；block 位置 distillation KL；scheduled sampling 從 teacher-forced 漸增到 own committed blocks。
關鍵 practice：corruption＋self-conditioning（NCM F.3：moderate 大幅改善、過量削弱 conditioning）；own-output distillation（CLLM Jacobi／Fast-dLLM v2）；block curriculum B=4→8→16→32；confidence-aware partial commit＋1–2 refinement pass（每 pass 計 t_trunk）；跨 block KV reuse。
**主失敗模式**：full-block commit 下能力崩潰——吐低熵 boilerplate 自我驗證通過但推理消失（force_4→0.413）。次要：「free verification」是循環的（adapted trunk 驗自己的分佈），品質必須用 task metrics vs 原模型把關，不是 accept rate；chat 熵使 ≥12 tokens@≤2% 基本不可能。

**3. Q3：算力（B200 ~2.25 PFLOPS BF16、35–45% MFU）**
8B 全 FT 1B tokens：6ND≈4.8e19≈15–25h；LoRA ~10–20h。27B 1B≈50–90h。teacher 資料（27B batched+MTP ~4–8k tok/s/GPU）：1B≈35–70h、2B≈70–140h。
8B gate：data 50＋adapt 20＋eval 10≈80h。R2 全程：3–4 次 8B 迭代 ~300h＋27B pilot ~280h＋buffer≈**800–1200h，2000h 裝得下，slack ~1.7–2.5×**。R1 ~500–1000h（裝得下但 ~1%）；R3 pretrain 50B≈750h/run＋teacher 1400–3500h（裝不下迭代）；R4 ~100–300h。**算力不是瓶頸，research bet 才是。**

**4. Q4：stage gates**
8B gate（≤100h）：Fast-dLLM v2 式 adapt Qwen3-8B，B16–32，~0.5–1B self-distilled；量 full-block commit（無 per-token verify）下 committed tokens/pass @≤2% rel loss（GSM8K300＋HumanEval164＋chat subset）。**GO：code & math ≥12–15、chat ≥8–10**（committed-at-quality 是續寫熵屬性、近乎 scale-independent，故可預測 27B）。**STOP：code <8 或 chat <5 @≤2%** → 預測 27B ~1.5–2×DFlash2，停項。frozen ratio_0.1 只 1.25×；joint training 要把 commit 4→12+@fixed quality，若只到 6–8（Fast-dLLM v2 級）就停。
27B pilot：adapt 27B ~1B tokens，B200 conc-1 vs DFlash2／JetSpec++。**GO：code ≥2.5× 且 chat ≥2× @≤2%；NO-GO：code <1.8× 或 chat 任一 >2% drop → 停。**

**5. Q5：一行**
**Stop**——bar 比無損前沿（JetSpec++ accept 8.94／SpecPilot 10.87×AR）高 ~3×、比 lossy adaptation 前沿（Fast-dLLM v2 2.5×AR）高 ~5×，2000h 買得到工程買不到突破；若 owner 堅持花，R2 過 ≤100h 8B gate 是唯一 ~5% 的賭注，否則停。
