Structure: with a frozen trunk, every round runs the trunk over the committed block anyway (to get the hidden states for the next draft), so verification is free; the only lever is tokens committed per trunk pass. speed = L_committed / (t_trunk + t_draft). For 3x DFlash2 at about 15-17 ms per round one needs about 22-25 committed tokens per round averaged over code/math/chat; chat is at 3 today.

Candidate routes for 2000 GPU-hours.
R1. Bigger lossless drafter: block 32-64 DSpine-style drafter trained much longer on 27B self-distilled data. Trunk frozen, output identical.
R2. Draftable trunk: LoRA (or full) fine-tune of the trunk jointly with a block decoder so the trunk's own continuation becomes predictable in blocks: the trunk's hidden state at a block boundary is trained to carry a "concept" that determines the next 16-64 tokens, decoded in parallel by a small decoder; the trunk reads the committed block in one pass (which also gives free per-position verification against the adapted trunk). R3. Native NCM student (multiscale VQ tokenizer + coarse-to-fine generator) distilled from the 27B, at 1-8B scale.
